Certify the Gate, Not the Generator: Certified Emission of Language-Model Claims with Cell-Complex Verification

Sole author. Manuscript, 2026.

Rather than trying to make a language model truthful, certify the gate that decides which of its claims reach a reader. The gate is calibrated with Learn-then-Test and betting p-values, and exact holonomy on a cell complex catches contradictions around cycles that pairwise checks miss.

On 81,414 MedLFQA claims it passes 77.8% at a 3% unsupported rate and 92.5% at 5%, with no violation in 100 random splits, against 24.3–35.5% for conformal factuality.

CELLO: Vision Transformers on Oriented Cell Complexes with Certified Early Exits

Sole author. Manuscript in preparation, 2026.

Each block passes messages between image patches, their adjacencies and 2 × 2 squares, so the model sees how patches are arranged, not only which belong together.

89.54% on CIFAR-10 at about 0.1 GMACs, and 90.61% with LocalViT’s convolution, against at most 88.56% for ViT and HgVT (one seed so far). Early exits certified with Learn-then-Test cut multiply-adds 2.18× and raise measured throughput 1.81×.

Perimeter: region tokens from an oriented cell complex

Sole author. Research in progress, 2026.

A vision transformer whose tokens are the regions of an oriented cell complex built for each image. One signed pass over the region borders recovers each region’s exact area, centroid and second moments, as in Figure 1.

In a CPU pilot on Imagewoof, region tokens beat patch tokens on all three seeds (64.7% vs 62.8% top-1) with 69% of their multiply-adds. An H100 study at 224 pixels is next.

Reading the Mind from the Body

Research proposal.

Feelings leave traces in the body: heart rhythm, voice, face, breath, skin and eyes. The study records them alongside deep-brain activity in people already undergoing epilepsy monitoring, maps which brain circuit drives which body signal, how strongly and how fast, and links each to the research dimensions of mental health (RDoC). The goal is an open readout model that learns from the brain, then runs on the body alone and says how certain it is.

Medera BH-ASR: speech recognition for behavioral-health conversations

Open benchmark from Medera, on Hugging Face.

English and Arabic clinical speech: voice activity detection, diarization, per-region language identification and word-level fusion of several recognizers, with a verifier and a hallucination guard. Seven animated explainers, captioned in English and Arabic, walk through its safety numbers.

Lowest word error rate of 13 recognizers on English mock consultations: 11.02% on PriMock57, level with Qwen3-ASR (p = 0.15) and ahead of Whisper large-v3 (13.55%). Under a medication prompt it inserted no drug names, where the prompted recognizer alone inserted 71.
